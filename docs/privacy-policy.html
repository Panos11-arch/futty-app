Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Futty ("the app", "we", "us") is a pitch-booking and 5-a-side-to-11-a-side football community app for Cyprus. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Information you provide

When you create an account and use Futty, you may provide:

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressAccount sign-in and password recovery
Username, full nameIdentifies you to other players/teammates
Phone number (optional)Only shown to other users if you turn on "Show my number publicly" — off by default
City, gender, date of birth, position, preferred foot, bio (optional)Shown on your player profile so teammates and opponents know who they're playing with

Information created by using the app

  • Bookings — which pitch, date, and time you book.
  • Teams and leagues — team membership, league standings, match results, XP and stats derived from games you play.
  • Squad posts — "need players" / "need opposition" posts you create or respond to.
  • Messages — chat messages sent between team captains to arrange a fixture. These are only visible to the two captains involved.
  • Follows — which players or teams you choose to follow.

Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token is stored so we can notify you about relevant squad posts. You can turn this off at any time in Profile settings, which removes your stored token.

Biometric app lock

Face ID / fingerprint app-lock is handled entirely on your device by iOS/Android's own biometric APIs. Futty never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data — the app is only ever told "unlocked" or "not unlocked".

Payments

If you purchase Futty Premium, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple (via the App Store) or Google (via Google Play). Futty never sees or stores your payment card details — we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, which we use to unlock Premium features on your account.

Who your information is shared with

  • Other users — your username, and any profile fields you've chosen to make visible, are visible to other signed-in users of the app (this is a community app; player/team browsing is a core feature). Your email and phone number (unless you opt in) are never shown to other users.
  • Service providers — the app is built on Supabase (database, authentication, hosting) and Expo/EAS (app infrastructure, push notification delivery). These providers process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Blocking and reporting

You can block another user from within the app, which hides their messages and posts from you. You can also report a user or piece of content; reports are reviewed by the app's developer.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove most profile fields at any time from Edit Profile.
  • You can turn off push notifications and phone number visibility at any time.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
