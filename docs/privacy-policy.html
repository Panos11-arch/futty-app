Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Futty ("the app", "we", "us") is a pitch-booking and 5-a-side-to-11-a-side football community app for Cyprus. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Information you provide

  • Email address — account sign-in and password recovery.
  • Username, full name — identifies you to other players/teammates.
  • Phone number (optional) — only shown to other users if you turn on "Show my number publicly", off by default.
  • City, gender, date of birth, position, preferred foot, bio (optional) — shown on your player profile so teammates and opponents know who they're playing with.

Information created by using the app

  • Bookings — which pitch, date, and time you book.
  • Teams and leagues — team membership, league standings, match results, XP and stats derived from games you play.
  • Squad posts — "need players" / "need opposition" posts you create or respond to.
  • Messages — chat messages sent between team captains to arrange a fixture, visible only to the two captains involved.
  • Follows — which players or teams you choose to follow.
  • Futty AI chat — messages you type into the in-app assistant, used only to answer your question.

Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token is stored so we can notify you about relevant squad posts. You can turn this off at any time in Account > Settings > Notifications, which removes your stored token.

Biometric app lock

Face ID / fingerprint app-lock is handled entirely on your device by iOS/Android's own biometric APIs. Futty never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data — the app is only ever told "unlocked" or "not unlocked".

Payments

Personal and Team Premium purchases are handled entirely by Apple (via the App Store) — Futty never sees or stores your card details, only confirmation that a purchase was made. Pitch booking payments (including each player's split share) are processed by Stripe; Futty never stores your full card number. We keep a record of what you paid for, how much, and its status (held, charged, refunded).

Who your information is shared with

  • Other users — your username, and any profile fields you've chosen to make visible, are visible to other signed-in users (this is a community app; player/team browsing is a core feature). Your email and phone number (unless you opt in) are never shown to other users.
  • Service providers — the app is built on Supabase (database, authentication, hosting), Stripe (pitch booking payments), Apple (App Store purchases), Expo/EAS (app infrastructure, push delivery), Sentry (crash and error reports, including technical device information) and Anthropic (processes Futty AI chat messages to generate answers). These providers process data on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party ad/tracking SDKs.

Contacts

If you allow it, the Community tab checks the phone numbers in your device's contacts against Futty accounts to show which of your contacts already use the app. The numbers are sent to our server only for that check and are not stored.

Blocking and reporting

You can block another user from within the app, which hides their messages and posts from you. You can also report a user or piece of content; reports are reviewed by the app's developer.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove most profile fields at any time from Edit Profile.
  • You can turn off push notifications and phone number visibility at any time.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Account > Settings > Delete account. This removes your login, profile details and saved cards. Match results you took part in stay in league tables under "Deleted player" so other teams' records stay accurate. You can also ask us to do this by email.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above.
