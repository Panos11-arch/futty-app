Common questions

How do I book a pitch?

Open the Book tab, pick a day, city, format, and duration, then tap an open slot on any venue to reserve it.

How do I find players or opposition for a game?

From your booking, or from the Players/Match quick actions on Home, you can post that you need more players or need an opposing team, and other users can respond.

How do league fixtures and scores work?

Once your team is registered in a league, fixtures are scheduled by agreement between team captains, and either captain logs the result after the match. If both sides' submitted scores match, the result is confirmed automatically.

How do I report a problem with another user?

You can block a user or report content directly from the app. If you need to escalate something urgently, email us using the button above.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at the address above and we'll remove your account and associated data.
