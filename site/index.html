FAQ

Questions before kickoff

What does a game cost?

The venue sets the pitch price, and it's split between the players. Futty adds a service fee of €0.80 per player when each player pays their own share, or €0.55 per player when one person pays for everyone. For example, a €60 5-a-side costs €6.80 each. You see every price before you pay.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay in the app. Where the venue allows it, you can pay cash at the venue; the service fee is still paid by card when you join.

Can I cancel?

Within 1 hour of paying, you get everything back. After that, until 6 hours before kickoff, you get your payment minus €1 as credit at that venue. Less than 6 hours before kickoff, or if you booked less than 6 hours before kickoff, there's no refund.

What if the venue cancels?

Every player gets their full share back, as a card refund or as credit at that venue. You choose within 48 hours.

What if fewer players join?

The full pitch price is always paid. If fewer players join, the players who joined split it, and the difference is charged after the match. You're told 6 hours before kickoff, so you can find more players. A player who holds a spot and doesn't turn up still owes their share.

What is Gipedo Pano?

Two teams play and the losing team pays for the pitch. A draw or a dispute splits it 50/50.

What if the weather is bad?

The app shows the forecast for your match. The venue can warn about bad conditions or cancel the game. If the venue cancels, you get your full share back.

What happens if I don't pay?

Money you owe becomes a debt to the venue or to Futty. Until it's paid, you can't book or join games anywhere on Futty.

Which formats can I play?

5-a-side to 11-a-side, and 6 v 6 at venues that allow it.
