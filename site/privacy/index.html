Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Futty ("the app", "we", "us") is a pitch-booking and 5-a-side-to-11-a-side football community app for Cyprus. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Information you provide

  • Email address: account sign-in and password recovery.
  • Username, full name: identifies you to other players and teammates.
  • Phone number (optional): only shown to other users if you turn on "Show my number publicly", off by default.
  • City, gender, date of birth, position, preferred foot, bio (optional): shown on your player profile so teammates and opponents know who they're playing with.

Information created by using the app

  • Bookings: which pitch, date and time you book or join.
  • Teams and leagues: team membership, league standings and match results.
  • Squad posts: "need players" and "need opposition" posts you create or respond to.
  • Messages: chat messages sent between team captains to arrange a fixture, visible only to the two captains involved.
  • Follows: which players or teams you choose to follow.
  • Futty AI chat: messages you type into the in-app assistant, used only to answer your question.

Match records

When you play a match booked or arranged in Futty, we keep:

  • Check-ins: whether you checked in, when, and how (you scanned the venue's code, your captain ticked you, or the venue marked you as arrived). Only players who checked in count in stats, man of the match, ratings and levels.
  • Goals, assists and lineups entered for the match.
  • Man of the match votes and the ratings (1 to 10) you give other players.
  • Your level and its history, worked out by Futty from results and ratings, and your answers to the level questions.
  • Stats: season stats, leaderboards and team of the week, worked out from confirmed matches.

Who sees them:

  • Nobody sees who voted for whom or what score one player gave another. You see only your own vote and your own ratings.
  • The match page (score, goals, lineups, each player's match rating once there are enough ratings, and the man of the match once voting closes) is seen by the players in the match, and by everyone signed in once the result is confirmed.
  • Check-ins are seen by the players in the match and by the venue's staff.
  • Levels, season stats, leaderboards and team of the week are seen by everyone signed in.
  • Your answers to the level questions are seen only by you.

What venues see

A venue's staff see, for their own venue only:

  • your bookings at that venue, and the players in them;
  • what you paid at that venue and how;
  • anything you owe that venue;
  • whether you checked in, and how many times you held a spot and didn't show up.

A venue never sees your bookings, payments, debts or no-shows at another venue.

When you open a venue's post-match offer, Futty makes a code for you. The venue sees what the code is for, how many codes were opened and which were used at its counter. These counts stay with the venue after you delete your account, but are no longer linked to you.

Players offered by a venue

A venue can offer a player who isn't on Futty to a team that's short. Staff type only that player's first name. There is no account, email or phone number, and Futty never contacts that player. The name is seen by the venue's staff, the booker, and, once the booker accepts, the other players in the booking.

Camera

If you allow it, Futty uses the camera only to scan the venue's check-in QR code at a match. The picture is read on your phone to find the code and is never saved or sent; only the code is sent to check you in. You can type the code instead.

Photos

Venue staff can choose photos from their phone to add pictures of their venue and its menu. Before the first upload, staff confirm the photos are the venue's and Futty may show them. Futty uploads only the photos staff pick, resized, and shows them to players in the app. Futty never reads players' photo libraries.

Contacts

If you allow it, the Community tab reads the phone numbers in your device's contacts and checks them against Futty accounts so it can show which of your contacts already use the app. The numbers are sent to our server only for that check and are not stored.

Calendar

If you choose to add a booking to your calendar, Futty writes that event to your phone's calendar and remembers, on your phone only, which event it added. Futty doesn't read your other events or send them anywhere.

Notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token is stored so we can tell you about your bookings, matches and squad posts. If you add your WhatsApp number and choose WhatsApp for reminders, we store that number and send those reminders through WhatsApp; reply STOP to stop them. You can turn each kind off at any time in Account > Settings > Notifications.

Biometric app lock

Face ID and fingerprint app lock is handled entirely on your device by iOS and Android's own biometric APIs. Futty never receives, stores or sends your biometric data; the app is only ever told "unlocked" or "not unlocked".

Payments

Futty Premium (personal and team) is bought through Apple's App Store. Pitch bookings are paid by card through Stripe. In both cases the payment provider handles your card details; Futty never sees or stores your full card number. We keep a record of what you paid for, how much, and its status (held, charged, refunded).

Who your information is shared with

  • Other users: your username, and any profile fields you've chosen to make visible, are visible to other signed-in users (this is a community app; player and team browsing is a core feature). Your email and phone number (unless you opt in) are never shown to other users.
  • Venues: as described in "What venues see".
  • Service providers: the app is built on Supabase (database, authentication, hosting), Expo/EAS (app infrastructure, push delivery), Stripe (card payments), Apple (App Store purchases), Sentry (crash and error reports, which include technical device information), Anthropic (processes Futty AI chat messages to generate answers) and Meta (delivers WhatsApp reminders, if you choose them). These providers process data on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Blocking and reporting

You can block another user from within the app, which hides their messages and posts from you. You can also report a user or piece of content; reports are reviewed by the app's developer.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove most profile fields at any time from Edit Profile.
  • You can turn off notifications and phone number visibility at any time.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Account > Settings > Delete account. This removes your login, profile details and saved cards. Match results you took part in stay in league tables under "Deleted player" so other teams' records stay accurate. You can also ask us to do this by email.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
