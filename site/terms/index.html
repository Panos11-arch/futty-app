Prices and the service fee

  • Each venue sets its pitch prices. The pitch price is split equally between the players the booking is for (10 for 5 v 5); any cents left over from the split go on the booker’s share.
  • Futty adds a service fee for each player: €0.80 when each player pays their own share, €0.55 when the booker pays for everyone in one payment. It is the same whatever the payment method.
  • Premium players pay no service fee on their own spot.
  • The app shows what you pay, worked out by Futty from the venue’s prices, before you pay.

Booking and paying

  • When booking, the booker chooses how the game is paid: the booker pays for everyone; each player pays their own share with the booker responsible for any spot left unpaid; or each player pays their own share and is responsible only for their own spot.
  • The booker must finish paying within 15 minutes of booking, or the booking is cancelled and its pitches released.
  • When you pay, your payment is held on your card and taken 1 hour later, or 6 hours before kickoff if that comes first. A payment made less than 6 hours before kickoff is taken straight away and can’t be refunded; the app asks you to accept that first.
  • Everyone who pays in the app saves a card, so that top-ups and anything you owe can be charged to it later.
  • When the booker is responsible, their card is held for the full pitch price 3 days before kickoff (at booking if the game is sooner). 2 hours after the match, anything still unpaid, service fee included, is taken from that hold and the rest released. If the hold can’t be placed, the game still goes ahead, and whatever it would have covered becomes the booker’s debt.
  • Whatever happens, the full pitch price is paid. If fewer players join than the booking is for, the players who joined split the full price, and the difference is charged to their saved cards 2 hours after the match. This doesn’t apply when the booker paid for everyone.
  • If you choose to pay cash at the venue, you pay the service fee by card when you join and your part of the pitch to the venue. If the venue hasn’t recorded your cash by collection, it is treated as unpaid.

Cancelling

  • Within 1 hour of paying, for a game more than 6 hours away, you can cancel and get everything back.
  • After that, until 6 hours before kickoff, €1.00 is kept and the rest becomes credit at that venue. Exactly 6 hours before kickoff still counts as before.
  • Less than 6 hours before kickoff, nothing is given back, even if your spot is filled.
  • A booker who cancels the whole team (each player paying) pays €1.00 for each other player whose payment was already taken, shown before they confirm; the other players get their whole share back. Less than 6 hours before kickoff nobody gets money back and there is no such charge.
  • A booker who paid for everyone can cancel only the whole booking. Until 6 hours before kickoff, €1.00 per player is kept and the rest becomes credit at that venue.
  • If the venue cancels, every player gets their whole share back, as a card refund or as credit at that venue. You have 48 hours to choose credit; otherwise it goes back to your card. The same choice applies when the booker cancels the whole team.
  • Nothing can be cancelled once the game has started. A player who cancels can’t rejoin the same booking.

Venue credit

  • Credit can only be spent at the venue that issued it, and can’t be cashed out or moved to another venue.
  • Credit expires 12 months after it is issued.
  • Credit pays your part of the pitch with no service fee. If it doesn’t cover it all, the rest is paid by card with the normal service fee. Credit can’t be used with cash.
  • Credit is held by the venue. Futty keeps the record of each player’s credit at each venue.

Money you owe

  • You owe a venue when a share you were responsible for is left unpaid, for example cash you didn’t pay at the venue or a top-up your card refused. You owe Futty when a charge for cancelling the whole team can’t be taken.
  • While you owe any venue or Futty, you can’t book, join a booking or choose cash anywhere on Futty, at any venue, until it is paid.
  • You can always pay what you owe in the app by card, with no extra fee, or settle it with the venue in person, which the venue then records.

Gipedo Pano

  • In Gipedo Pano the losing team pays for the pitch. Each team’s captain has their card held for the pitch price plus €0.55 for each player in the booking.
  • After the match, the losing team’s hold is taken in full and the winning team’s released. A draw or a disputed result splits it: half of each hold is taken and the rest released.
  • Gipedo Pano can only be booked up to 5 days ahead. A team that pulls out 6 hours or more before kickoff pays €1.00 per player from its hold; less than 6 hours before, it counts as the losing team.

Premium

Personal Premium costs €3.99 a month, as an auto-renewing subscription through the App Store, under Apple’s terms as well as these. Premium players can book up to 14 days ahead instead of 7 days, and pay no service fee on their own spot. Cancel it in your Apple account’s subscription settings; Premium lasts until the end of the period you paid for.
